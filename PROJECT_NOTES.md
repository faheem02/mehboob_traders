## BUSINESS FLOW NOTES
- Suppliers: mal dene walay. Purchase cash ya credit. Supplier balance = due on purchases - cash paid.
- Customers: retailers. Sale cash ya udhaar. Customer balance = due on sales - cash received.
- Both run on CREDIT + CASH (thora/pooora). paid_amount + due_amount on every purchase/sale.
- Unit tracking: pcs/dozen/box/carton/kg/pack/gadda.
- Stock: purchase adds stock, sale reduces stock. Low-stock & out-of-stock alerts.
- Cash flow: purchases/expenses/supplier payments = cash OUT. sales/customer receipts = cash IN.
- Bank flow: payment_method=bank uses bank account, else cash.
- Purchases have invoice_no PUR-yymmdd-###. Sales have INV-yymmdd-###.
- Customer_no format CUS-yymm-####.
